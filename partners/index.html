For sponsors

Put your name on a school’s new facility.

Every facility we build can carry a sponsor: a name on the building, a wrap on its walls, and robots from our partner RobotLab appearing at games. Our parent company, Stadia IP, sells and manages these sponsorships, and the money helps the school pay for the facility.

We’re pre-seed and haven’t built a facility yet. The first sponsorships will be tied to our first builds in 2027.

What sponsors can buy

The market we’re building for, starting in Virginia: roughly 21,000 U.S. high schools and colleges, 10,000 parks and recreation departments, 1,000 concert and event venues and 500 pro and minor-league venues.

Facility naming rights

“The [Your Brand] Performance Center.” Your name on the building and on the school’s schedule.

Exterior wraps and signage

Container walls make good billboards: roughly 190 sq ft per side on a 20′ unit and 380 sq ft on a 40′.

RobotLab robot appearances

Through our partner RobotLab, robots carrying your brand can appear and perform at games.

Fan packages

Fan-funded programs and experiences that connect your brand with the families, students and alumni in the stands.

Where your money goes

Sponsor money doesn’t just buy exposure. It goes toward the cost of the facility, so a school can add seating, training rooms or locker space it couldn’t otherwise afford.
