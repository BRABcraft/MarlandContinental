Future markets

Designs beyond athletics

The same container approach we use for bleachers and training rooms could work on bases, in disaster zones, on school campuses, at festivals and on city streets. These are early concept designs. Athletics comes first; we’ll look at these markets after our first builds.

Why containers fit these uses

What we learn building for athletics should carry over: the same steel shells, the same shop process and the same three-tier approach to cost.

  1. 1

    Easy to ship

    A standard container moves by truck, rail or ship. The mobile gym concept is sized to fit on a C-130.

  2. 2

    Tough steel shell

    Shipping containers are built to cross oceans, which makes them a durable starting point for shelters, labs and stores.

  3. 3

    Built in a shop, movable

    Units are finished indoors, set on site and can be moved again when the need changes.
