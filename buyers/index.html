FAQ

Good questions.

What sizes do your facilities come in?

Every design is built from 20′ or 40′ high-cube shipping containers. Larger facilities, like the stacked bleacher unit, stacked VIP club and container stage, combine two modules with decks, canopies and stairs.

Can a facility be moved later?

Yes. Our facilities are factory-built and relocatable, so they can move with your program, or to a new field, when your needs change.

How does Stadia IP funding work?

Stadia IP packages the facility’s intellectual property (naming rights, sponsorships and robot appearances) into sponsor-ready revenue. That revenue helps offset the capital cost of your facility. We’ll walk you through what’s realistic for your program during your consultation.

Where do you deliver?

We’re starting in Virginia and serving the Mid-Atlantic (Virginia, North Carolina, Maryland, D.C. and Delaware), all within trucking range of our Portsmouth home base. Our warehouse is also rail-served.

Can we rent instead of buying?

Yes. We offer season and event rentals for programs that need space but aren’t ready to purchase.

What do the prices include?

Prices are per unit and delivered, including a freight and setting allowance. They don’t include site foundation (a concrete pad typically runs $4,000–$12,000, or $1,500–$5,000 for a pier system), utility service to the pad, sales tax or local building permits.

Do you offer package pricing?

Yes. Buying several units for one site, like bleachers, a press box and a concession stand, qualifies for package pricing. Ask us for a multi-unit quote.
