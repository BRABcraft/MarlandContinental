What athletic directors keep telling us

When we talked to Virginia athletic directors, the same three problems kept coming up.

Not enough space

Storage, locker rooms and training space run out years before a new building can be approved, funded and built.

Construction is expensive

Permanent buildings mean architects, permits and long schedules, all paid for up front.

Sponsorship rarely covers it

Naming rights often go unsold or under-priced, so they don’t make much of a dent in a project’s cost.

Three tiers on every design

Every facility comes in Economy, Standard and Luxury. The footprint and layout stay the same; the materials and equipment change.

Economy Median design ~$31K

Durable, practical finishes for programs that need the space first.

  • Painted steel framing
  • Bench seating, laminate counters, wire shelving
  • Fixed-height tables and budget equipment
  • Meets code and health-department minimums
Standard Median design ~$63K

Upgraded materials and commercial-grade equipment.

  • Hot-dip galvanized exposed steel
  • Fold-down seating, solid-surface counters, full slatwall
  • Commercial equipment and upgraded glazing
  • Built for daily team use
Luxury Median design ~$114K

Higher-end finishes for donor, sponsor and premium game-day spaces.

  • Thermally broken, low-E glazing
  • Chairback seating, stone bars, custom millwork
  • Electric hi-lo tables and premium-brand equipment
  • Ipe decks, keypad entry and full climate control

Questions

Have you built any of these yet?

Not yet. We’re a pre-seed startup. We’ve designed and priced 17 facilities, surveyed Virginia athletic programs, and leased a warehouse at 500 Confederate Ave in Portsmouth. Our first builds are planned for winter 2026–27, with the first deliveries in 2027.

Are the pictures real?

No. Every product image on this site is a concept rendering made to show the design. Once we build our first units, we’ll replace them with photos.

How accurate are the prices?

They’re estimates from our cost model, which prices the materials, equipment, labor and freight for each tier. They’re per unit and delivered, including a freight and setting allowance. They don’t include site foundation (a concrete pad typically runs $4,000–$12,000, or $1,500–$5,000 for a pier system), utility service to the pad, sales tax or local permits. Your written quote sets the final price.

What sizes do the facilities come in?

Every design is based on 20′ or 40′ high-cube shipping containers. Larger ones, like the stacked bleacher unit, stacked VIP club and container stage, combine two containers with decks, canopies and stairs.

Can a facility be moved later?

Yes. They’re built in our shop and set on site, so they can be picked up and moved to a new field or campus.

How does sponsorship funding work?

Stadia IP, our parent company, sells naming rights, wraps and sponsorships for sports venues. If you opt in, they’ll look for a sponsor for your facility, and that money goes toward its cost. Whether a sponsor signs on, and for how much, depends on your program and market, so we’ll talk through what’s realistic before you commit.

Where will you deliver?

We’re starting with Virginia, North Carolina, Maryland, D.C. and Delaware, all within trucking range of Portsmouth. Our warehouse is also rail-served.

Do you offer package pricing?

Yes. Buying several units for one site, like bleachers, a press box and a concession stand, qualifies for package pricing. Ask us for a multi-unit quote.
