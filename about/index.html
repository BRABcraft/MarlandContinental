How we got here

We started by talking to athletic directors. Most told us the same things: they’re out of space, new buildings cost too much and take years, and sponsorship money rarely covers a project.

Shipping containers looked like a practical answer. They’re strong, relatively cheap, easy to move and can be finished indoors. So we designed 17 athletic facilities around them, from bleachers to locker rooms, and priced each one in three tiers.

Bill already runs Stadia IP, which sells naming rights and sponsorships for venues. Pairing the two means a school can ask whether a sponsor could cover part of the cost. It won’t work for every facility, but it’s an option most builders don’t offer.

What’s different about us

Plenty of companies modify containers. We only design for athletics, and we can bring a sponsorship option with us.
