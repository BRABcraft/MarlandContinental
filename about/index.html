Our story

Every school needs more space. Few can fund it.

We started by listening to athletic directors. They told us the same three things: they’re out of space, new buildings cost too much and take too long, and sponsorship dollars rarely cover the cost of a project.

So we built a different kind of facility company. We convert shipping containers into bleachers, press boxes, training rooms, concessions, locker rooms and VIP suites, and price every design in three tiers so every budget has an option.

Then we solved the funding problem. As part of the Stadia IP ecosystem, every facility we build comes with naming rights and robot sponsorships that help pay for it. We don’t just quote a price. We show you how the facility pays for itself.
