Why build with containers?

Athletic programs often outgrow their buildings years before a new one can be approved, funded and built. A finished container facility skips most of that process.

About us
  1. 1

    Lower cost

    Estimated delivered prices start around $17K. Every design comes in three tiers, so you choose the finish level that fits your budget.

  2. 2

    Built in a shop, set on site

    We plan to build from 20′ and 40′ high-cube containers in our warehouse, deliver by truck or rail, and set each unit on a pad or piers. It can be moved later.

  3. 3

    Optional sponsorship funding

    Our parent company, Stadia IP, sells naming rights and sponsorships. For some facilities, that could cover part of the cost.

How sponsorship funding would work

Marland Continental is part of Stadia IP, which sells naming rights and sponsorships for sports venues. For schools that want it, Stadia IP would look for a local sponsor for the new facility, and the sponsor’s money would go toward its cost. How much it covers depends on the facility, the school and whether a sponsor signs on, so treat it as a possible bonus, not a promise.
