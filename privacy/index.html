Privacy

Last updated October 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you send through marlandcontinental.com. If anything here is unclear, email bill@stadiaip.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms:

  • Quote requests (on each design page): your name, organization, role, email, phone, delivery state, timeline, notes, and the design, tier and options you picked.
  • Contact form: your name, email, organization, phone, message, what you’re interested in, and whether you want updates.

With each form we also record the time, the page you sent it from, and your browser’s user-agent string (which browser and device type you used).

We don’t use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare quotes, and, if you checked the box, send occasional updates about the company. We don’t sell your information or share it with advertisers.

If you ask about sponsorship funding, we may share your organization’s name and the facility you’re interested in with Stadia IP, our parent company, so they can look for sponsors. We’ll tell you before sharing anything beyond that.

Where it’s stored

Form submissions are saved to a private Google Sheet that only our team can access. Google stores that data under its own privacy policy.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like any web host, GitHub and Google may log basic request information such as your IP address when your browser loads the site.

Deleting your information

Email bill@stadiaip.com and we’ll delete your submissions or stop sending you updates.
